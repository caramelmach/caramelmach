### Selected Projects

#### [🗺️ WorldPin](https://github.com/caramelmach/WorldPin)

A personal world map for Android and iOS. Pin the places you've been or want to go, sort them into categories, attach photos and browse them in a full-screen gallery with pinch-to-zoom.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TypeScript` · `Reanimated` · `Gesture Handler` · `Expo Image`

#### [📓 Paperly](https://github.com/caramelmach/paperly)

An offline desktop notebook with rich text and freeform design pages, custom covers and PDF export. It saves your work automatically and keeps a backup.

`Electron` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Tiptap` · `Konva`

#### [💸 Harcama Takip](https://github.com/caramelmach/harcama-takip)

A personal expense tracker. Add expenses by category and see your monthly total and a category breakdown. Data is stored on the device.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TypeScript` · `AsyncStorage`

#### [🔥 Alışkanlık Takip](https://github.com/caramelmach/aliskanlik-takip)

A daily habit tracker with streaks, a 7-day view and a daily progress bar.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TypeScript` · `AsyncStorage`

#### [🌤️ Hava Durumu](https://github.com/caramelmach/hava-durumu)

A weather app that needs no API key. It has city search, current conditions and a 7-day forecast with temperature range bars.

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Open-Meteo API`
